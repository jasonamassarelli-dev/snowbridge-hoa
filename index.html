About Our Community

Welcome to Snowbridge Square

Snowbridge Square is a homeowner-owned condominium community situated in Center Village at Copper Mountain Resort in Summit County, Colorado. Our prime ski-in / ski-out location puts residents and guests steps away from world-class skiing, dining, and mountain recreation year-round.

Many owners enjoy the flexibility of renting their units through a variety of vacation rental companies, making Snowbridge Square a popular destination for mountain visitors.
